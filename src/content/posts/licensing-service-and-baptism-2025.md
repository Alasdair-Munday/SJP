This was a significant Sunday in the life of St John's Park.

The church family gathered to give thanks as Revd Luke Graham was licensed as Priest-in-charge. The service also included a baptism, making it a joyful day of commitment, welcome, and prayer for the next chapter of ministry in Park.

It marked a moment of stability and hope as the church continued growing in worship, mission, and community life.
