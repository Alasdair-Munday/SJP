Alpha returned to St John's Park in February 2026.

The course created a weekly space for food, conversation, and honest questions about life, faith, and Jesus. People were encouraged to invite friends, neighbours, and anyone who wanted to explore Christianity in a relaxed setting.

Alpha is one of the simple ways we help people take a next step in faith.
