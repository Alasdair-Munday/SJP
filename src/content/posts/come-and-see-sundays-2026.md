During May, every member of the church family was invited to come and see what happens in children's church.

The invitation was about valuing children's ministry, understanding where church resources are going, supporting the team, and inspiring new volunteers. It sat alongside other children and families moments, including Stay and Pray, the Bright Lights event in Rotherham, and the children's Toilet Twinning fundraiser.

We are grateful for the children, young helpers, adult volunteers, and families who make this ministry such a joyful part of church life.
