Christmas at St John's Park was full of food, worship, creativity, and welcome.

The weekend included Jingle Bell Jollof, our church Christmas party with food, games, singing, and dancing. On Sunday we gathered for a shorter communion service, followed later by Christingle and Carols as a family outreach service.

On Christmas morning we came together again to worship and celebrate the birth of Jesus.
