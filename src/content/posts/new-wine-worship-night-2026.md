St John's Park hosted the New Wine Worship team for a worship night in March 2026.

The evening gathered people from across the city to seek God together in worship and prayer. Tickets were fully booked ahead of the night, and the church family helped welcome guests, steward the building, and make space for a full gathering.

It was a special moment of worship beyond our usual Sunday rhythm.
