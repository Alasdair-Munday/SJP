This Advent, we're hosting **Honest Advent**, an art exhibition by **Scott Erickson**, at St John's Park.

Christmas can come with a lot of expectations. Life is often more complicated: joy alongside grief, changed plans, difficult relationships and new beginnings. Honest Advent invites us to pause and discover the wonder of God meeting us in the real world.

Through the artwork of Scott Erickson, this exhibition explores the extraordinary truth that God stepped into the mess, struggle, and beauty of human life; not a perfect world, but a very real one. 

The artwork is placed throughout the Church building, as well as some pieces on display in the churchyard.

Each image has a reflection available by QR code and a passage of scripture. 

We hope these art stations help you pause and contemplate the sacred mess and miracle of Immanuel ’God With Us’ this advent.
