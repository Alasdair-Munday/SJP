This Advent, we're hosting **Honest Advent**, an art exhibition by **Scott Erickson**, at St John's Park.

Christmas can come with a lot of expectations. Life is often more complicated: joy alongside grief, changed plans, difficult relationships and new beginnings. Honest Advent invites us to pause and discover the wonder of God meeting us in that very real world.

The exhibition explores the vulnerability of the incarnation: God becoming human in Jesus and entering the mess, struggle and beauty of human life. These images invite us to look again at the Christmas story and ask where God might be meeting us today.

Each art station includes **a passage of Scripture and a reflection available by QR code**. Take time to look, read and reflect as you move through the exhibition.

Whether Advent feels hopeful, busy or difficult this year, you're welcome to come with your questions and make space to contemplate **Immanuel — God with us**.
