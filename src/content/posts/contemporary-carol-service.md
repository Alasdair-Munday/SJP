On Sunday 20th December at 4pm we'll be joining together to sing carols and have fun! It will be an afternoon of classic carols, dancing, joy and reflective moments. In the service we'll be journeying through the Christmas story by looking at bible passages and listening to a short talk. At the end of the service there will be refreshments and mince pies - we would love to see you there!
