A group of us recently attended the Diocese of Sheffield Conference, where we explored practical strategies for helping churches grow. The three days included teaching, workshops and time to connect with church leaders from across the diocese.

It was also encouraging to see Linda, one of our young people, helping to lead a workshop and prayer meeting alongside young people from across the diocese.

One of the most encouraging things was recognising that St John’s is already putting many of these ideas into practice—and that we are beginning to see real fruit and growth as a result.

We are looking forward to sharing what we have learned with the wider church and discerning together the next step God is calling us to take as a community.
