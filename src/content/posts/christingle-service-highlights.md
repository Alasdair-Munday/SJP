Thank you to everyone who helped host and lead this special service.

Christingle is one of the ways we love gathering people of different ages together around the story of Jesus. It gave us space for singing, prayer, reflection, and a sense of shared celebration at the close of Advent.

If you are new to St John's Park, we would love to welcome you on a Sunday morning.
