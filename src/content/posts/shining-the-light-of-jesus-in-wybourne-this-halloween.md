## Happy Halloween!

we are taking to the streets of the Wybourn estate to use this opportunity to share the love of Jesus through a friendly greeting and the message of the gospel. We will be handing out some fantastic resources designed to share the Gospel in a friendly and accessible way during Halloween and we hope that people will know that they are loved and hear the invitation to explore the Gospel message.

## Event Info

We will be meeting at 3:45 to pray on the corner of Wybourn house road and Cricket Inn Terrace and will be serving hot chocolates between 4 and 6 PM Even if you can only come for a short time, please swing by and say hello! If you are planning to come and help please email luke@saintjohnspark.org to let Luke know.

## Partnering with Arise and The Diocese of Sheffield

This is part of a city wide initiative combining "Arise:Hallow" by Arise Sheffield and the Diocese of Sheffield's "Shine your light Fortnight".

For more information on Arise:Hallow - https://www.arisesheffield.org/hallow-26

For more about the Shine your Light Fortnight: https://www.sheffield.anglican.org/your-calling/lights-for-christ/seasonal-resources/shine-your-light-fortnight/
