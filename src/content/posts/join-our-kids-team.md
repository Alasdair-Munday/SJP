St John's Park has a range of youth and Children's groups, made possible by a small army of team but we are particularly asking people to consider Joining the team for our younger age groups:

## Park Tots

Park Tots is our group for pre-schoolers run by Claire Smith. This learning-through-play group meeting in the creche room involves imaginative creative play bursting with opportunities to introduce our youngest members to the goodness of God through simple crafts, games and stories. Currently parents are required to stay with their children but if a few more people join and learn how to deliver the sessions, we will be able to provide parents with space to stay and listen to the sermon while our children learn about the goodness of God in ways they can understand.

## Park Kids

Our group for reception to year 5 Children is designed to give our primary aged Kids space to reflect on the difference Jesus wants to make in their friendships, school and home life. As well as have a shed-load of fun! Every session includes games, a Bible story, craft and a short lesson about following Jesus in their world.

**We would love you to join this team** because it will mean our Kids will be **inspired** by the way that Jesus is at work in your life and will enable us to provide a healthy, sustainable ministry that continues to nurture our precious Kids' faith for years to come.

We've recently reduced the age range for this group, meaning that the group is more focussed on a specific age group and allows us to move at a pace our younger members can follow.

We have a handful of fantastic team that make this group happen but they are scheduled more regularly than they would like. They are excited to work with you to help you build your skills and confidence in running groups for this age group.

## Please Pray

Please pray for our teams and all the children and young people at St Johns' Park that they would know the Goodness of the Father, The transforming power of the Spirit and the life in its fullness that Jesus offers them.

And please prayerfully consider Joining the Kids team. Even if you're not certain its for you, Kirsty would love to hear from you and chat through how you might be able to support the ministry.
