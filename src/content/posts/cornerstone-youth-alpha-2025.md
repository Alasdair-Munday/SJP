Cornerstone continued to grow as a place for young people to explore faith and build friendships.

During autumn 2025 the group worked through Alpha together, with space for questions, discussion, prayer, and honest conversation about following Jesus. The course finished in December and became part of a wider pattern of youth discipleship, worship, and monthly evangelism.

We are thankful for the young people, leaders, and helpers who made the term such an encouragement.
