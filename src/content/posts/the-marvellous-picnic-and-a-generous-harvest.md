We had a wonderful time visiting Emmaus School for their Harvest Service assembly just before half term. Kirsty, our Children and Families Minister, helped a group of children bring the story of Jesus feeding the 5,000 to life with a performance of *The Marvellous Picnic*! Our very own Reverend Luke Graham also shared a thought from the Bible passage, helping everyone reflect on this amazing story of generosity and God’s provision.

And the generosity didn't stop there! The school families brought in a fantastic donation of food, which was shared between our Monday Foodbank and S2 Foodbank. It was a beautiful reminder that, just like the little boy who shared his picnic, even small acts of giving can make a big difference. What a Marvellous Picnic indeed!
