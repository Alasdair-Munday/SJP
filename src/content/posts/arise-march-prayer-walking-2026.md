In March we joined with Arise March, a city-wide invitation for Christians to prayer walk the streets of Sheffield.

People were encouraged to pray as they moved around the city, gather with friends, and intentionally pray for their own streets and neighbourhoods. The prayer walking app helped churches across Sheffield see how much of the city was being covered in prayer.

It connected our local rhythm of prayer with the wider church across the city.
