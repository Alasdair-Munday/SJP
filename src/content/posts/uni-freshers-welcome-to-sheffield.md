Thank you to Scott, Christian, Victor and Leah for representing us at Sheffield Hallam Church Search - An opportunity for new students who have moved to our city to find a church.

If you're a student please [get in touch](https://stjohnspark.org/contact/)

There are blocks of Hallam acommodation in our parish and we pray regularly that all the students that live there will know the love of God and the joy of Belonging to his family. We would love to welcome some more students to join our growing number of students and young adults who call St John's Park home.

Please look out for any students who might be visiting church in the coming weeks and consider having an invite and an extra Sunday lunch portion ready for any students who would appreciate a warm meal in a welcoming family home.
