**St John's Park, Sheffield · 14 hours per week · £20.65 per hour**

Could you use your nursing skills to help people find support, build confidence and care for their health?

We're looking for a registered nurse to establish a new parish nursing service for people in **Hyde Park, Park Hill, Wybourn and Manor Oaks**. Two years of grant funding are in place to help this ministry take root.

Our vision is to see **Eden in Park**: a neighbourhood where God's restoring life helps people flourish. Parish nursing will be a practical expression of that vision, offering care that recognises people's physical, emotional, social and spiritual needs.

## About the role

You'll begin with a weekly Monday health drop-in alongside our food bank and Lunch Club, providing a welcoming place to talk through health concerns and take the next step towards support.

Working alongside NHS and community services, you'll:

- Offer preventive health advice, education, advocacy and help accessing appropriate services.
- Build relationships with residents, particularly people living with long-term health conditions, isolation or barriers to care.
- Offer spiritual care sensitively, guided by each person's wishes.
- Develop activities shaped by local needs, with possibilities including health education, movement and mobility groups, and mental health or addiction support with appropriate partners.
- Explore outreach through local schools, housing providers and community organisations, and equip volunteers to sustain suitable activities beyond the initial funding.

The working pattern includes the Monday Community Hub clinic, 10 am–12:30 pm, and the Tuesday staff meeting, 9–11 am. The remaining hours can be arranged flexibly with the team around the needs of the right candidate.

We'll agree realistic priorities together within the role's 14 hours a week. The service will be open to **people of all faiths and none**, with no expectation of attending church or taking part in religious activities.

## Who we're looking for

You'll be a nurse with current NMC registration and relevant experience, able to work independently, build trust and work well with others. You'll share our Christian vision for this ministry and respect each person's beliefs and choices. Being a practising Christian is an occupational requirement for the post.

You'll need to have completed Parish Nursing Ministries UK's Preparation for Parish Nursing Practice course or be willing to undertake it. We'll fund the course if required; entry requires at least two years of professional practice. Professional support will be available through PNMUK, alongside local line management and spiritual supervision.

The role is based at **St John's Church, Bernard Street, Sheffield S2 5PU**, with local outreach as the service develops. We anticipate launching the service in April 2027, subject to recruitment and training.
