# Maranatha: Come Lord Jesus

Advent is a time where we prepare our hearts for the coming of Jesus and his kingdom. Every year we gather for our Maranatha prayer and worship night to pray together for Jesus' kingdom to come.

## Timetable

Come for as much or as little of the evening as you can.

- **4–5:30pm — Family prayer and worship:** All-age worship, creative prayer activities and hot dogs.
- **5:30–6:30pm — Reflective prayer space:** A calm space to explore the prayer stations at your own pace.
- **6:30–7pm — Prayer for the parish:** Particularly around food security.
- **7–7:30pm — Honest Advent:** Reflections on the Honest Advent artwork.
- **7:30–8pm — Worship**
- **8–8:30pm — Prayer for schools, families and young people in the parish**
- **8:30–9pm — Worship**
- **9–9:30pm — Prayer for Nigeria**
- **9:30–10pm — Prayer for the city:** Praying for the church in the city and for evangelism.
- **10pm–midnight — Free prayer and worship**

## Worship

We will be joined by worship leaders from across the city who will lead us in times of extended worship, declaring the greatness of God and crying out for his kingdom to transform our lives and our world.

## Prayer

### Sessions

Through the night, members of our congregation will be leading us in times of focused prayer for Nigeria, Our local Schools, The church in the city, Local communities, Our political leaders and more.

### Prayer stations

There will be prayer stations to help us pray in creative ways throughout the church. Some of these will be based on the focus of our prayer sessions and some will be reflections based on the Honest Advent art gallery that we are hosting in the Church throughout Advent.

## Want to get involved?

If you would like to lead a prayer session please email al@saintjohnspark.org and we can have a chat!
