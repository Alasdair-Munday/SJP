Across summer 2025, our children and families ministry helped host a series of community moments for families and neighbours.

The year included a sports-themed Bible holiday club, a summer fair, and a special ice cream-themed Sunday service. These events helped children, parents, volunteers, and local families share time together in a relaxed and welcoming setting.

We are grateful for everyone who planned, served, welcomed guests, made food, ran activities, and helped create space for people to feel at home.
