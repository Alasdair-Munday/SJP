Our wardens were commissioned during a special service at Sheffield Cathedral on Wednesday 10 June.

We are delighted to welcome Jack Dudgeon as one of our churchwardens and grateful to Bess Cooper for continuing to serve for another year. Churchwardens play a vital role in supporting the life and ministry of the church, and we are thankful for their commitment to our church community.

Please keep Jack and Bess in your prayers as they help lead and serve St John's, working alongside our clergy and congregation to ensure our church remains a safe, welcoming, and flourishing place for all.
