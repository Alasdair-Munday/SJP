We will gather as a church to explore the vision of our summer festival. We will explore three visions of heaven in the bible:

Restored Community

Restored Creation

Restored Culture

We will be learning about how being in Christ requires us to delight in the world God has created and live lives that proclaim his goodness.



There will be talks, discussion and children's groups. Breakfast and lunch will be provided.
