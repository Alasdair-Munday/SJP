We meet at 10:45am every Sunday. Come as you are; you do not need to know the words or the format before you arrive.

The first Sunday is **All In Communion**, where every generation stays together. The third Sunday is **Holy Communion**. On the second, fourth and fifth Sundays we share a **Service of the Word**, with age-specific groups during the second part of the service in term time.

There is time for refreshments and conversation afterwards.
