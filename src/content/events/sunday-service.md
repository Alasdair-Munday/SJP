We meet at 10:45am every Sunday. Come as you are; you do not need to know the words or the format before you arrive. There is always plenty of tea, coffee, cake and conversation after the service so plan to stick around.

## First Sunday - All In Communion
The first Sunday is **All In Communion**, where every generation stays together through the service. The sermon, music and other activities are accessible for everyone. It's alot of fun!
## Second, fourth and fifth Sundays - worship and a talk.
On the second, fourth and fifth Sundays we share a **Service of the Word**, with age-specific groups during the second part of the service in term time.
## Third Sunday - Holy Communion
The third Sunday is **Holy Communion**. The kids have groups during the prayers and the talk and then return to join the grownups so we can share communion together as a whole church family.
