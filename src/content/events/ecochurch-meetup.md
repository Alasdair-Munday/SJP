  discuss how we can be a community that prioritises our responsibility to care
  for creation.
location: In the Church Office
timeText: Straight after the service on the 4th Sunday of the month.

  src: /images/uploads/church-flower.jpeg

Join us as we discuss how we can use our resources in a way that minimises our impact on the environment and how we can use our green space to draw our neighbours into fully appreciating the precious gift of God's creation.
